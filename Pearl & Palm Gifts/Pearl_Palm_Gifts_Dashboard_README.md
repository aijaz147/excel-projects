
# Pearl & Palm Gifts - Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents a **Sales & Performance Dashboard** for **Pearl & Palm Gifts**, a UAE-based gifting store.  
The dashboard tracks **key business KPIs**, visualizes sales performance, and provides insights into customer behavior, sales channels, delivery trends, and payment methods.

---

## Business Problem
The management team at **Pearl & Palm Gifts** needed a **centralized performance view** to:
- Track **Total Sales, Orders, and AOV** (Average Order Value).  
- Compare **revenue contribution across channels and occasions**.  
- Monitor **delivery performance and on-time rate**.  
- Understand **payment preferences and customer demographics**.  
- Identify **top-selling products** for better inventory planning.  

---

## Key KPIs
The dashboard highlights the following KPIs:
- **💰 Total Sales:** $514,082  
- **🛒 Total Orders:** 4,993  
- **📈 Average Order Value (AOV):** $103  
- **👩 Female Orders:** 48.09% | **👨 Male Orders:** 51.91%  
- **🌱 New Customers:** 8.21% | **🔄 Returning Customers:** 91.79%  

---

## Dashboard Features
1. **Monthly Sales Trend** → Line chart to show seasonality & revenue spikes.  
2. **Top 5 Products by Revenue** → Bar chart to highlight best-sellers.  
3. **Revenue by Occasion** → Bar chart for seasonal demand (Eid, Corporate, Weddings, etc.).  
4. **Revenue by Sales Channel** → Compare performance of Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.  
5. **Delivery Type Breakdown** → Orders by Next-Day, Same-Day, Scheduled.  
6. **Payment Method Share** → Donut chart (Card, COD, Wallet).  
7. **On-Time Delivery Rate** → Donut chart showing late vs on-time orders.  

---

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Slicers & Filters  
  - Custom Formatting (K/M values)  
  - KPI Cards with conditional formatting  
  - Donut & Bar Charts  

---

## Insights
- **Website & WhatsApp dominate sales** (>$300k combined).  
- **Corporate & Eid gifts** are the most revenue-generating occasions.  
- **Late Deliveries (71.5%)** show a serious operational issue.  
- **Returning customers (~92%)** show strong brand loyalty.  
- **Card Payments (59.5%)** dominate, but COD remains significant (27.9%).  

---

## Business Impact
This dashboard provides:  
✔ A **360° view of sales performance**.  
✔ Helps identify **bottlenecks in delivery**.  
✔ Guides **marketing campaigns by occasion & channel**.  
✔ Supports **data-driven decision-making** for management.
