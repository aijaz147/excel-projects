# Sales Insights Dashboard – Summit Outfitters (2023–2024)

## 📌 Project Overview
A professional Excel dashboard for a U.S. retail brand, **Summit Outfitters**, built to analyze sales data from 2023–2024.  
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights using interactive KPI cards, slicers, and modern visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Summit Outfitters needed:
- A central place to track total sales, orders, returns, and B2B performance.
- An easy way to view seasonal sales trends and demographic breakdowns.
- Instant visibility into top-performing cities.

---

## 💡 Solution
I developed an **interactive Excel dashboard** that:
- Consolidates 9,600 records into a single reporting tool.
- Tracks **5 key KPIs**: Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Return Rate %, B2B Orders %.
- Filters data by **Gender**, **Month**, and **Order Status**.
- Visualizes trends and breakdowns with consistent, professional chart designs.

---

## 📊 Features
- **Dynamic KPI Cards** – auto-update when slicers change.
- **Monthly Sales Trend** – identify peak and low sales months.
- **Age Group & Gender Analysis** – understand customer demographics.
- **Top Cities by Sales** – see high-value markets instantly.
- **Order Status Distribution** – delivered, returned, refunded, cancelled.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
  - PivotTables & PivotCharts
  - GETPIVOTDATA for dynamic KPIs
  - Slicers & Timeline
  - Color palette design & layout formatting

---

## 📈 Insights Gained
- November–December had the highest revenue (holiday season).
- Senior customers placed more orders on average than other age groups.
- Sacramento, Milwaukee, and Dallas led in total sales.
- Return rate remained stable at around 5%.

---

## 🚀 Impact
- Reduced reporting time from **3 days to seconds**.
- Improved executive decision-making with on-demand analytics.
- Provided a clean, modern reporting tool.

---

## 📂 How to Use
1. Open `SummitOutfitters_SalesDashboard.xlsx` in Excel.
2. Use slicers to filter by Month, Gender, or Status.
3. Review KPIs and charts for insights.
