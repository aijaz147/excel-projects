# Velvet Bean Cafe Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
The **Velvet Bean Cafe Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence tool designed to analyze sales performance for a specialty coffee shop operating globally from **2021 to 2024**.
It enables stakeholders to track trends, compare performance across countries, and identify top customers to support strategic decision-making.

## Objectives
- Provide a **clear and interactive view** of sales trends over four years.
- Compare **sales performance by coffee type, roast type, and pack size**.
- Identify **top-performing countries and customers**.
- Allow stakeholders to explore data dynamically using slicers and timelines.

## Dashboard Features

### Filters / Slicers
- **Country** – Select specific regions (e.g., United States, UK, Australia, etc.)
- **Size** – Filter by coffee pack size (0.2 kg, 0.5 kg, 1.0 kg)
- **RoastType** – Filter by roast level (Light, Medium, Dark)
- **CoffeeType** – Filter by coffee bean type (Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, Excelsa)
- **Timeline** – Select specific months or years between 2021–2024

### Key Visuals
1. **Total Sales Over Time** – Multi-line chart showing sales trends for each coffee type.
2. **Sales by Country** – Horizontal bar chart ranking countries by total sales.
3. **Top 5 Customers** – Highlights the most valuable customers based on sales.

## Insights & Findings
- **United States** leads all countries with **$76,988** in total sales.
- **Robusta** consistently outperforms other coffee types over time.
- Sales show **seasonal peaks** during colder months, with dips in mid-year.
- **Top customers** significantly contribute to revenue, with the top five generating over **$2,300** combined.

## Tools & Techniques
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, pivot tables, slicers, charts, dashboard design
- **Data Visualization** – Trend lines, comparative bar charts, KPI tracking
- **Interactive Filtering** – Slicers and timelines for user-driven exploration
